  WHY commit_sha DIFFERS (by design, documented in REPORT.md):
  The pipeline stamps metrics.json with HEAD at the time `dvc repro` runs, so a
  reproduction on a later commit always records that commit. The VALUES are the
  contract; the SHA documents which checkout produced the numbers. Pattern:
  code commit -> dvc repro -> commit lock+metrics.
